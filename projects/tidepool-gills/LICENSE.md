## Third-party DSP

The physical-model voice is derived from Mutable Instruments Braids by Emilie
Gillet and the Axoloti/Ksoloti `osc/brds/brds_digital.h` adaptation by Johannes
Taelman. The granular player is derived from Mutable Instruments Clouds by
Emilie Gillet.

Tidepool also links the Clouds and Braids resource tables included with Ksoloti.
